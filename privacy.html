Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Short version: Ties has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your relationships stay on your devices and in your own private iCloud. We never see them.

What Ties stores

The people you add, how you categorize them, moments you log, notes, promises, important dates and your settings. This data is stored on your device. If iCloud sync is on, it is also stored in your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can access. You can turn sync off in Settings → Intelligence & privacy.

Contacts and Calendar

With your permission, Ties reads your contacts to let you choose who to import, and your calendar to recognize meetings with people you know. This happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except your own iCloud, and Ties never writes to your contacts or calendar.

Intelligence

Suggestions, search and summaries run on your device, using Apple's on-device models where available. Relationships you mark as private are never analysed. Nothing is sent to us or to third-party AI services.

Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Widgets and Live Activities read a small summary that Ties keeps on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Ties contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not collect personal data. If you opt in to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide anonymized diagnostics to us.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all data from your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Ties → Delete Data.

Children

Ties is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: Support.
